The Case of the Borrowed Alibi

A novel by Ralph M. Butler and Opus

A classic Perry Mason formula — bought alibis, courtroom traps, Della and Paul running the case with Perry. The characters are drawn from the Raymond Burr television series of the late 1950s and 1960s rather than the Gardner paperbacks. A modern twist: the reader follows the investigation with the ‘Columbo’ license of seeing the pieces fall into place — the thrill isn’t just the ‘Who,’ but the ‘How.’

Summer 1971. A Hollywood stuntman walks into Perry Mason’s office with a confession that could end his career — and send him to prison. He’s been selling alibis for a powerful studio producer’s Tuesday nights, and now that producer’s wife has been found strangled in their Hollywood Hills home.
