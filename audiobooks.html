Audiobooks

An experiment — synthetic narration

These are experimental audiobook versions of some of the novels. They are a side project and may be removed at any time — please don’t treat them as permanent.

Nobody read these aloud. The narration is generated by VoxCPM2, an open text-to-speech model, run locally on Apple Silicon (via MLX). The narrator voice is a zero-shot clone of a neutral, public-domain reading clip from LibriVox (the “smith” voice). The result is clear but even-keeled — it reads calmly, even through tense scenes.

Files are in M4B format, which keeps chapter markers and works in audiobook apps (Apple Books, and most podcast/audiobook players). Clicking a link downloads the file.
