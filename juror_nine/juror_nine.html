Juror Nine

A novel by Ralph M. Butler and Claude Opus

This short novel is an attempt to match the style of Elmore Leonard in a fun story about a hitman that ends up on the jury for his target.

Dan Mercer teaches history at a Nashville university and quietly works as a hitman on the side. When a local contract lands on his desk, the target gets arrested for murder before Dan can act — and then Dan is summoned for jury duty on the same trial. He’s seated as Juror Nine. His target is at the defendant’s table.
