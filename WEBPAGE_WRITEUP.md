This novel brings the classic Perry Mason formula — bought alibis, courtroom traps, Della and Paul running the case with Perry. The characters are drawn from the Raymond Burr television series of the late 1950s and 1960s rather than the Gardner paperbacks. While it honors the courtroom traps and high-stakes investigations of Erle Stanley Gardner, it adds a modern twist: the reader follows the investigation with the "Columbo" license of seeing the pieces fall into place. The thrill isn’t just in the "Who," but in the "How"—watching Mason, Street, and Drake dismantle a perfect crime before a jury’s eyes.

----

Summer 1971. A Hollywood stuntman walks into Perry Mason’s office with a confession that could end his career—and send him to prison. He’s been selling alibis for a powerful studio producer’s Tuesday nights, and now that producer’s wife has been found strangled in their Hollywood Hills home.

Hamilton Burger has an airtight case: a $500,000 insurance motive, a private security code known to only a few, and a paid-for alibi that the producer can’t admit is a lie without admitting to murder. But Mason suspects the truth is buried deeper than a simple domestic betrayal. To save his client and find the real killer, Perry, Della Street, and Paul Drake must navigate the backlots of Argonaut Pictures, untangling a web of studio embezzlement and long-held secrets.
